## 🚇 Logistyka bazowa
- **Genua Airport → Galea Studio:** Volabus lub taxi do Genova Piazza Principe, dalej krótki spacer do Vico Dora
- **Galea Studio → Porto Antico / akwarium:** spacer ok. 10-15 minut
- **Galea Studio → Portofino:** spacer do Genova Piazza Principe, pociąg do Santa Margherita Ligure, dalej prom
- **Luton → easyHotel Old Street:** DART/shuttle do Luton Airport Parkway, pociąg do St Pancras, dalej Northern Line lub taxi
- **easyHotel Old Street → puby Arsenalu:** Northern Line + Piccadilly Line albo szybki taxi ride do Holloway Road / Highbury
- **easyHotel Old Street → Stansted:** Liverpool Street + Stansted Express
